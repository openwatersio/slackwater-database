# JMA Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Add JMA harmonic stations with reproducible source evidence.
**Architecture:** Pinned HTML and SHA-256 manifest, offline importer, provider-specific parser, existing quality and merge rules.
**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Node, Vitest, existing engine and datum packages.
**Spec:** ../specs/2026-11-02-jma-design.md

## Constraints and review focus

Preserve zeros and source precision. Fail on unknown constituent speeds, malformed coordinates, missing pages or unexpected withdrawals. Keep source longitude separate from rounded inventory coordinates. Do not conflate nearby JHOD gauges. Validate seasonal terms over multiple seasons.

Round 2 completed with Claude Opus and Claude Sonnet. Regression tests cover the relay’s own UHSLC metadata, conflicting operator targets, non-example station drift, crosswalk completeness, attribution, century-crossing years and older prediction snapshots.

Ruling: Store datums relative to MSL, retain the published tide-table zero as CD, and use CD as the height reference. NLLW remains derived separately. The two levels differ by up to 0.098 m across all 70 stations; they must not be conflated. Datum regeneration preserves MSL zero and published CD.

Ruling: A relay’s own UHSLC metadata outranks a sibling’s declaration. Accepted segments lacking their own metadata follow a superseded sibling to its operator. Conflicting targets fail closed. The same gauge rule applies to every provider-qualified group.

Ruling: Keep the measured worker bundle guard at 7 MiB, reserving 3 MiB under the paid-plan deployment limit. The guard is intended to flag future data growth.

Ruling: The JMA credit cites the station-list dataset page; individual constants URLs remain on the records. The source notes also identify the separate statutory restrictions flagged by JMA. No conclusion about a consumer’s forecast-service obligations follows from the copyright license.
