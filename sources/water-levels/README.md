# Water-levels lake fits

The importer fits harmonic constants and reduces datums from agencies' own observations in the Open Waters [water-levels lake](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels), and writes them as each agency's records: `data/smhi/<series>.json` for SMHI and `data/wsv/<series>.json` for the WSV's PEGELONLINE gauges. These records sit beside TICON-4's records of the same gauges, and the [quality evaluation](../../docs/quality-evaluation.md#duplicates) chooses which one each gauge publishes. An agency joins `CONTRIBUTORS` ([lake.ts](lake.ts)) once its lake series are fully backfilled, in the order of the lake's [TICON replacement inventory](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/blob/main/docs/ticon-replacement.md), and `sources/<agency>/metadata.yaml` declares its credit. `CONTRIBUTORS` is keyed by the lake source (`smhi`, `pegelonline`), and each entry names its `agency`: its TICON suffix and its `data/` directory (`smhi`, `wsv`), so a fit ranks as the agency's TICON relay of the gauge does.

```sh
npm run import -w sources/ticon        # first: the gauges and their names come from TICON
npm run import -w sources/water-levels
```

## Gauges

The gauges are the agency's TICON records. The lake lists GESLA's record of each under the TICON record's name (`gesla/<id>`), and that record's `preferred` names the agency's series, such as `smhi/2130`. A gauge whose GESLA record the lake prefers to the agency's own series, or prefers another agency's series for, has no record here. A record names its TICON record in `source.gauge`. It takes the TICON record's name and position too, except where its contributor sets `lakeMetadata`. WSV records take PEGELONLINE's name and position for the gauge instead: TICON's WSV names drop their umlauts and spaces (`Bsum` for Büsum, where PEGELONLINE has `BÜSUM`, which the catalogue title-cases), and at St. Pauli and two Baltic gauges the only TICON record is CMEMS's, which isn't licensed for commercial use. Gauge codes the title-casing can't know, such as `UP`, `AP` and `MPM`, are curated names in the station files. `source.gauge` is how the quality evaluation knows the two describe one gauge.

## Reading

[`readLakeSamples`](../../packages/harmonic-analysis/lake.ts) (`@slackwater/harmonic-analysis/lake`) reads a series' year files with DuckDB, puts every datum epoch in the current datum with its `to_current`, and by default keeps only values the agency has verified: flag good, not preliminary, interpolated, suspect or bad. Fits read the final values (`FINAL_FLAGS`): verified ones, and preliminary ones from before the station's `source_qc_start` in the lake's station list, which the agency will never check. A source whose `finalizes` is false in the lake's `sources.json`, such as PEGELONLINE, never marks a value checked, so all its preliminary values count as final. Elsewhere, where the lake knows no such date, preliminary values stay out of the fit, as they do after it. The held-out year is scored on the same values. Datums read every measured value, verified or preliminary. Only values on the hour are read: PEGELONLINE's 1-minute series would give sixty times the samples and no more tide. A period with values but none on the hour is an error rather than a short series; every SMHI and PEGELONLINE series is on the hour. Which year files a series has comes from its source's year list, `stations/years/<source>.parquet`, so a year the list has but the lake can't serve is an error rather than a gap, and a station the list marks as being written, or one with observations but no listed years, can't be read until the lake catches up. The list is checked again once the files are read, so a read fails if the lake wrote the station's files meanwhile: the lake marks a station before it writes any of its files ([Which years a station has](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/blob/main/docs/data.md#which-years-a-station-has)). A year list with an epoch that the station list doesn't have yet is an error too. A source without a year list can't be read. Files are cached in `tmp/water-levels/`. A year file is fetched again only when the year list gives it a new version; the station list and the year list are revalidated by ETag on every read. Only the public lake is read. The lake corrects the clock errors the TICON importer fixes from GESLA (`LOCAL_TIME_SOURCES`, `EARLY_GAUGES`), so this importer applies neither.

## Epoch

Each fit holds out the year that starts the day after its TICON record's last day and is fit over the 19 years before that year, one nodal cycle. Neither the fit nor TICON's record of the gauge has seen the held-out year, so the comparison is fair to both, and the fit covers the same period as TICON's record. Another TICON record of the gauge can run later, and then it has seen part of that year. A gauge with less than half of that year to score, such as an old gauge TICON covers to its last reading, holds out its last calendar year or the one before. A TICON record fitted on that year can lose to the fit there but can't beat it. Each gauge's window follows its TICON record, so the fits don't share one pinned epoch.

## Fitting and scoring

The fit takes the TICON record's constituent names, with `fitHarmonics`, as the TICON importer's GESLA re-fits do. The fit and every TICON record the lake files under the same series, such as SMHI's, CMEMS's and UHSLC's copies of the gauge, then predict the held-out year. Each is scored by `tidalResidual`: the RMS of observed minus predicted after a Godin filter (24, 24 and 25-hour running means) takes out weather and the datum, leaving the diurnal and faster band where those constituents' errors show. `source.held_out` records the year, the hours scored, the fit's score, and for each TICON record its score, the standard error of the difference and a digest of the constants scored. The standard error comes from 30-day blocks of the year, which weather leaves roughly independent. A gauge with fewer than half its held-out hours, or too short a fit window to analyze, gets no record.

The scores are compared in the quality evaluation, not here. A fit loses only to a TICON record it scores worse than by more than a constituent error of `AMPLITUDE_TOLERANCE` (3 mm) would add to the residual's variance (A²/2) and more than twice the standard error, and then it, and every record that scored no better than the best such record, is a duplicate of that one. Otherwise every TICON record of the gauge is a duplicate of the fit, which comes from the agency's own series and can be refitted. A record the lake files more than 10 km from the gauge isn't compared. Against the gauge's other records, the fit ranks as its agency's TICON relay. The score can't see the long-period constituents (SA, SSA, MM and the rest), which the filter removes, so a fit keeps its TICON record's, or another TICON record's of the gauge where `MISFILED_RECORDS` lists its own (Uddevalla's is Arkö's data), or the fit's own where the lake files no other that is licensed for commercial use, and the [seasonal contamination](../../docs/quality-evaluation.md#seasonal-contamination) check applies to it as to a TICON record. When the TICON record's constants have changed since they were scored, the digest no longer matches, the scores decide nothing, and the fit's quality issues say to re-run this import. Fits are scored as they are published, rounded to 1 mm and 0.01°.

## Datums

Datums are reduced over the same years, the fit and held-out years, from verified and preliminary values alike, under the rules in [docs/datums.md](../../docs/datums.md), in the series' current datum (RH 2000 for SMHI, each gauge's zero, PNP, for the WSV). A mean over more months outweighs the checks an agency has yet to make, and much of an older SMHI series is preliminary and may never be verified: at Uddevalla, verified values cover 12 months of the window and all values 36. `epoch` is the fit period.

## Records

Records have `published_harmonics: false`: these constants are fitted here. The source is not authoritative, since these are Open Waters' fits of the agency's data rather than its own analysis. Each run fits the whole series the lake lists, apart from datum epochs the lake can't put in the current datum, so a refit replaces the saved one even when it has fewer samples, as after the lake re-flags values, and logs the drop. A gauge the lake can't fit keeps any record it has, unless `FORCE_HARMONICS=1`, which removes it.

## Limitations

- The lake's QC marks any value unchanged for six hours suspect. At SMHI's older, centimetre-resolution gauges that drops real readings on calm Baltic days, and with them the complete months an observed datum reduction needs ([water-levels#90](https://github.com/openwatersio/water-levels/pull/90)).
- A gauge without a TICON record has no record here, because the gauges, their constituent lists and their held-out years come from TICON records.
- A contributor's `agency` is its `data/` directory, so one whose directory already holds another source's records, such as DMI's, needs another name first.
- The importer runs by hand; no workflow refreshes `data/smhi` or `data/wsv`.
- The lake doesn't link GESLA's CMEMS copies of Rostock, Wangerooge, Kiel-Holtenau and Lübeck to their PEGELONLINE gauges, so those copies aren't scored against the WSV fits. The quality evaluation's own duplicate rules still decide between them by position.
- At Cuxhaven Steubenhöft and Husum, PEGELONLINE's history download and its live feed disagree by up to 6 and 8 cm (the lake's `pegelonline-download-api-differ` erratum). The fit, held-out and datum years all end by July 2026, before the lake's live values start, so they read only the download.
- A gauge's fit and its datums come from two reads of the lake. Each is complete and consistent with itself, but the lake can write the station between them.
